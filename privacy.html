Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

About This Project

ViceWire GTA is an independent gaming fan project using software tools to prepare and manage content for its own social media accounts.

Information We Access

Our automation may request permission through Google OAuth to upload videos to an authorized YouTube channel.

We do not request Google account passwords. This website does not provide a registration form or directly request personal information.

How Information Is Used

Authorized access is used to operate the connected channel and upload video content. Access is not used to sell personal information or deliver personalized advertising.

Storage and Security

Authentication credentials are intended to be stored in restricted development environments or encrypted automation secrets, not in public website files.

Third-Party Services

Our tools may interact with YouTube and GitHub. These services have their own privacy policies.

Access Revocation

Google account owners can review or revoke application access through their Google Account security settings.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when our tools or data practices change.
